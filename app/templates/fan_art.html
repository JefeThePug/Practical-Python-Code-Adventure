Fan Art Submissions

Practical Python Code Adventure welcomes fan art inspired by its stories, characters, puzzles, and the worlds within them. Whether it's a drawing, painting, animation, or video, we'd love to see how the community brings these adventures to life.

How Can I Submit My Art?

To have your work considered for display on the site, please send me an email include the following:

  • Display Name
    • The name you'd like displayed alongside your work.
  • Artwork
    • An image or video of your creation.
  • Description
    • A brief description of what your artwork depicts or the inspiration behind it.
  • Art Portfolio (Optional)
    • A link to your online art portfolio※, if you have one.

A few guidelines to keep in mind:

  • Submissions must be original creations inspired by Practical Python Code Adventure or its content.
  • ※ Only dedicated art portfolios will be accepted as portfolio links. General personal websites, GitHub profiles, programming projects, and other non-art portfolios will not be included.
  • Submissions will be reviewed before being featured on the site.
  • By submitting your work, you grant permission for it to be displayed on Practical Python Code Adventure with the provided display name and description. You retain ownership of your creation.
